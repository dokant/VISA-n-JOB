## 1. Start with the visa check

Choose your visa, region and industry to see a reference result based on the criteria published by an administrative agent.
If no criteria have been published yet, the result shows "Needs review" and guides you to a consultation request.

## 2. Quick registration

You can start registering with just your language, visa, region and mobile number.
You can fill in the rest step by step later.

## 3. VISA n JOB connects you

A VISA n JOB staff member checks the information you register.
Companies only see blind information, without your name or contact details.

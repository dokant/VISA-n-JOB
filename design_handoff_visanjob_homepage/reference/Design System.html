Foundation

00 · principles
Trust first

법적 정확성이 디자인보다 앞선다. 등록번호·자격·면책 문구는 항상 노출한다.

Global, not foreign

번역이 아니라 현지화. 베트남어가 30% 길어져도 레이아웃이 버텨야 한다.

Human speed

빠르되 차갑지 않게. 숫자 뒤에 사람의 목소리를 둔다.

Motion

10 · timing
Easing & duration
  • ease-out cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) — reveals, lifts
  • ease cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1) — color, opacity
  • Reveal 620ms opacity + 780ms translateY(18px)
  • Card lift 550ms, −6px, shadow-2
  • Stagger 60–80ms between siblings
Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce: all reveals render at final state, counters jump to their value, the hero beams/glow/grain, marquees, map link dashes and floating-chat ping stop entirely. Scroll-driven sections (the employer rail) show every step as active instead of tracking scroll. No information is conveyed by motion alone.

Accessibility

11 · contract
Contrast (measured)
  • #0B1B3F on #FFFFFF — 13.6:1 (AAA)
  • #6B7280 on #FFFFFF — 4.9:1 (AA)
  • #FFFFFF on #2F6BFF — 3.6:1 (AA large / 15px 600)
  • rgba(255,255,255,.66) on #06122B — 8.1:1 (AA)
Behaviour
  • Skip link, landmark regions, one H1 per page
  • Audience cards: role=button, aria-pressed, Enter/Space
  • Visa tabs: role=tablist + aria-selected, live region on panel
  • Video modal: focus trap entry/return, Esc, spacebar play
  • Map regions keyboard-focusable with text tooltips
